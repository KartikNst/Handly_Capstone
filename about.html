About Handly

Welcome to Handly, your premier destination for authentic handmade crafts and artisanal products from across India. We are passionate about preserving traditional crafts while empowering local artisans to showcase their incredible talents to the world.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, Handly began as a small initiative to help local artisans sell their products online. Today, we have grown into a thriving marketplace that connects thousands of artisans with customers who appreciate authentic, handmade items.

Our journey started when we realized that many talented artisans were struggling to reach customers beyond their local markets. We wanted to create a platform that would not only help them sell their products but also tell their stories and preserve traditional crafts for future generations.

Our Mission

To empower Indian artisans by providing them with a global platform to showcase and sell their handmade products, while preserving traditional crafts and promoting sustainable practices.

Our Values

Authenticity

Every product on our platform is genuinely handmade by skilled artisans, ensuring you receive authentic traditional crafts.

Empowerment

We believe in fair trade practices and ensuring artisans receive fair compensation for their incredible work.

Sustainability

We promote eco-friendly products and sustainable practices that protect our environment and support local communities.

Preservation

We are committed to preserving India's rich craft heritage by supporting traditional techniques and artisans.

Impact

Through our platform, we have been able to:

  • Support over 5,000 artisans across India
  • Preserve 50+ traditional craft forms
  • Generate sustainable income for artisan families
  • Promote eco-friendly and sustainable products
  • Create a global appreciation for Indian crafts

Join Our Community

Whether you're an artisan looking to sell your products, or a customer who appreciates handmade crafts, we invite you to join our community. Together, we can preserve traditional crafts and support local artisans.
